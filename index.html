Why Origami?

Inspired by 折り紙 – the Japanese art of paper folding – Origami Linux symbolizes transformation, creativity, and simplicity. Just as a single sheet of paper can be folded into infinite forms, Origami lets you shape your system to fit your unique workflow.

Atomic & Secure

Immutable OS with signed container images and atomic updates

Container-First

Develop in isolated environments while keeping your host clean

Beautiful by Default

Getting Started

Fresh Installation

Download the ISO and install fresh on your machine.

Standard ISO Nvidia ISO

Rebase from Fedora Atomic

Switch your existing Fedora Atomic system to Origami.

Show Commands 
# Rebase to Origami (standard)
sudo bootc switch registry.gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/origami:latest

systemctl reboot

sudo bootc switch --enforce-container-sigpolicy registry.gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/origami:latest

systemctl reboot

# For Nvidia systems
sudo bootc switch registry.gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/origami-nvidia:latest

systemctl reboot

sudo bootc switch --enforce-container-sigpolicy registry.gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/origami-nvidia:latest

systemctl reboot

Security & Verification

All images are signed with Sigstore for supply-chain security.

Verify Image Signature 
curl -O https://gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/-/raw/main/cosign.pub
cosign verify --key cosign.pub registry.gitlab.com/origami-linux/images/origami:latest
